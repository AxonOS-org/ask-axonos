# What I could not answer

Questions visitors asked that the agent had nothing to say to. This page
is written by whoever asked, not by us.

Most projects have this list and keep it private, discover it late, and
hear it from someone who has already decided not to engage. It is more
useful in the open: a gap here is a fact about this project's own
documentation, and it is the shortest description of what to write next.

A question appears after two different people ask it. One person asking
something is a question; two is a gap. Questions that might be about a
person, or that carry contact details, are never published — the filter
is deliberately over-broad, and losing a real gap costs less than
publishing somebody's circumstances.

**Refusals that are not gaps are not here.** The agent declines to state
a classification accuracy because accuracy is on the explicit non-claims
list. That is a position the project holds, not something it does not
know, and putting it on this page would turn one into the other.
